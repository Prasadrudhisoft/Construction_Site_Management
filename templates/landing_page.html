How RudhiArch ERP Works

A complete, role‑based workflow for modern construction management

1

Admin Setup & Assignments

Admin registers users, assigns sites, links projects to architects and accountants, and sets up organizational details.

2

Architect Design & Compliance

Architect uploads design details, structural plans, material specs, drawings, cost estimation, and legal compliances. PDFs auto‑generated.

3

Project Manager Execution

Project manager submits daily progress reports, worker counts, expenses, vendor quotes, and generates invoices. All notify admin.

4

Accountant Financial Management

Sets base salaries, processes monthly salaries with advance deductions, manages bills/payments, and generates reports with PDF output.

5

Admin Oversight & Approvals

Reviews and approves invoices, expenses, vendor quotes, legal documents. Real‑time notifications and chat keep every stakeholder aligned.

System Architecture & Real‑Time Flow

See how data and responsibilities move through the ERP – from creation to execution

1. Admin Creates Users

Registers Project Managers, Architects, Accountants with role‑based access.

2. Admin Assigns Site to Project Manager

Creates a site and links it to a Project Manager.

3. Admin Assigns Architect to Project MANDATORY

Only after this assignment, the project becomes visible to the architect, project manager, and accountant. This is the starting point of all further work.

4. Architect Submissions

Uploads design details, structural plans, material specs, drawings, cost estimation, legal compliances → auto‑PDF generation.

5. Project Manager On‑Site Work

Submits daily progress, worker reports, expenses, vendor quotes, generates invoices – all notify admin.

6. Accountant Processes Finances

Base salary setup, monthly salary (with advance deduction), bills, salary reports – PDF generation.

7. Admin Approves & Monitors

Approves invoices, expenses, vendor quotes, legal documents. Real‑time notifications keep everyone aligned.
